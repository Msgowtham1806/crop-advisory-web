Pick a district, a crop and a monsoon scenario. KrishiDSS estimates what the land will yield, what it will cost to grow, and whether the price will clear that cost — from published government records, not guesswork.

Free, no sign-in needed. Records end in 2015 — this is a demonstration on historical data.

The finding that shapes everything
73%
of yield variation is between districts, not between years within one district.
Soil, irrigation and local practice decide most of the outcome. That is why the monsoon setting moves the risk score sharply but the yield figure only a little — and why the model is built around each district's own record.
